Calculation: retained proposals ÷ eligible proposals × 100. Overall uses the total counts; it is not an average of the area percentages. “Retained” means MAIN / mainlisted for funding after evaluation, rather than a signed grant. “Eligible” excludes ineligible, inadmissible, duplicate and withdrawn proposals and proposals without evaluation results.

Organisation applications are different from proposals: one consortium proposal may contain several organisation applications. We use proposals consistently for both numerator and denominator.
