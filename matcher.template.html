What makes a match?

Transparent ranking

60 points for project-idea term coverage, 30 for requested expertise and 10 for researcher-profile terms. Without expertise selections, the weights are 80 / 0 / 20. Rarer terms carry more weight. Every component uses the same three cited projects. This is a retrieval heuristic, not a trained or validated prediction model.

Complementary evidence

Expertise labels expand into a published English keyword dictionary. An organisation must appear in a relevant project; it must also have evidence for at least one selected expertise area. Uncovered areas are shown, rather than assumed.

Validate before inviting

Check the actual researchers, work packages, current capacity, conflicts and call-specific eligibility. Co-participation does not establish a direct working relationship. Researchers shown here have not endorsed these recommendations.

Data scope, dates and reproducibility

Only non-terminated RIA, IA, CSA and joint-undertaking equivalents with at least three listed organisations are included. This corpus rule does not establish consortium eligibility. Individual ERC projects are excluded. Historical projects can be useful evidence; the optional timing filter checks scheduled dates only.

Researcher summaries and search tags were manually derived from official institutional profiles. No publication author matching has been performed. The sample call’s partner roles are our interpretation of its outcomes, not mandatory requirements. All dates and requirements should be verified against the official call.
